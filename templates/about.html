About

A working record, not a résumé.

SpencerLab is a self-hosted technical journal. Infrastructure gets stood up, faults get diagnosed, lab systems get pentested and assessed — and each one is written up honestly: the commands, the wrong turns, and why each fix mattered.

Write-ups are published straight from two in-house tools — AuditForge for assessment reports and Fieldnote for build notes — through a token-gated, secret-scanned publishing API.
